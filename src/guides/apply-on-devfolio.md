Devfolio runs many hackathons in India and across the Web3 community. Its process depends on the hackathon's mode: some events let you build and submit straight away, while others review applications first and ask accepted teams to confirm their place. Knowing which mode you are in saves a lot of confusion.

This guide follows Devfolio's own participant guide. Each event can add its own rules, so read the event's overview page too.

## Before you start

- **Read the overview page.** It sets eligibility, themes, prizes, dates and any extra steps.
- **Check both deadlines.** Devfolio events can have an application deadline and a later project submission deadline. On hackathon.so, the registration deadline is shown on each event page with a conversion to your time zone.
- **Create or update your Devfolio profile.** Some application answers can be pre-filled from it.

## Find out the hackathon mode

Devfolio describes three modes:

| Mode | Application review | Team | Extra steps |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Online | None | Solo or team | Submit during the submission period |
| Online with application review | Yes | Required | Accepted members RSVP; organisers check you in |
| Offline | Yes | Required | Accepted participants must attend the venue in person |

For online hackathons, Devfolio's guide notes you need a team record to submit, even if you work alone, and you can create or join a team any time before you submit.

## Applying to a hackathon with application review

### Step 1: Open the application

Find the hackathon on Devfolio, or click the **Apply with Devfolio** button on the event's own website, and log in.

### Step 2: Complete the application

Answer the organisers' questions. Mandatory questions are marked with a red asterisk. Some answers may be filled in from your profile. Keep going until the progress bar reaches 100%.

### Step 3: Choose how to apply

**Applying alone.** Click **Submit individual application**. Your application is then ready for review by the organisers. You will still need to join or create a team before you can submit a project.

**Creating a team (team admin).** Click **Join or create team**, enter a team name and click **Create Team**. Share the invite code with your teammates.

**Joining a team.** Complete your own application to 100%, click **Join or create team**, enter the invite code from your team admin and click **Join Team**. Check the team details and members.

### Step 4: Submit the team application

Once everyone has joined, the team admin submits the team for review. Remind your admin to do this before the deadline.

### Step 5: RSVP if accepted

If your team is accepted, you receive an RSVP window. Every team member must RSVP and be checked in before the submission deadline. If one member does not, the team cannot submit a project.

### Changing teams

- If you applied alone and want to join a team, withdraw your application first, then reapply using the team's invite code.
- To switch teams, withdraw your current application and reapply with the new team's code.
- While your own application is still being submitted, you cannot join a team that is already in "accepted" status. Devfolio suggests applying individually, then joining the team after you are accepted.

## Submitting your project

The steps are the same in every mode, once you are allowed to submit.

1. Log in and open the hackathon from your dashboard.
2. Click **Add Project**.
3. Fill in the project in the editor, which shows a live preview.
4. Save it as a draft, or click **Publish Project** to submit. Drafts show as **In Draft**; published projects show as **Submitted**.

If you are in a team, only the team admin can submit the project, so make sure every member has been added first.

### What the form asks for

- Project name
- Tagline (a one-line pitch)
- The problem it solves
- Challenges you ran into
- Technologies used
- Links, such as a repository, a live site or a design file
- A video demo (YouTube preferred)
- Screenshots (the first image becomes the project's cover)

If you are aiming for a sponsor or API prize, Devfolio's guide recommends naming those tools clearly in **Technologies used**, so judges can find your project.

### After submitting

Submitted projects become publicly visible on the hackathon page straight away and appear on your Devfolio profile. You can open drafts from your dashboard and use **Edit project** to make changes; teams can submit any time before the deadline.

## Tips

- **Finish your application early.** Teams can only form once applications are complete, and review takes time.
- **Agree who the team admin is.** That person submits the team application and the project.
- **RSVP as soon as the window opens.** One missing RSVP blocks the whole team.
- **Publish a complete draft before the final hour.** Uploads and last-minute edits take longer than you think.

When winners are published on Devfolio, they appear on the matching event page on hackathon.so. See [how judging usually works](/guides/how-judging-usually-works/).
